Keybinds

Click a key, then press the new one. Escape leaves it as it is. A key that is already in use trades places. Spells, targeting, the pet, Reset, the pull timer and zooming also take a key with Shift, Ctrl or Alt held, the middle mouse button, the thumb buttons, and the wheel with a modifier.

Mouse: hold right to steer, hold left to look around, both buttons walk forward, the wheel zooms (Zoom in and Zoom out can have keys as well). Click a dummy to target it. The arrow keys always move you. "Edit bar" moves spells between slots. A few combinations belong to the browser and never reach the game (Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T, Ctrl+N, Ctrl+Tab, and Ctrl+1 to 9 in some browsers).

Controller: the left stick walks, the right stick turns and looks. A B X Y cast slots 1 to 4; with the left trigger held slots 5 to 8, with the right trigger 9 to 12, with both 13 to 15. LB jumps, RB takes the next dummy. D-pad: up pull timer, down stop casting, left pet follow, right pet attack. Start resets the fight, Back opens the review. While you use it the slots show its buttons. Menus still take the mouse.

        Forever Warlocking

        Touch controls The stick on the left walks. Drag across the picture to turn, pinch to zoom, tap a dummy to target it. Tap a spell to cast it; hold a finger on a spell or a buff to read it. Tap your DPS for the fight review. Everything else is under Menu. Tap to close

        Fight review

        Challenges

        Fights with one thing to practise. Each is graded against what the sim reaches in the very same fight: S from 100%, A from 95%, B from 88%, C from 78%. Your own fight settings stay as they are; "End" at the top of the screen takes you back to them.

        Today's fight

        A new one every day, the same for everyone: the dice are fixed by the date (a day in UTC). Play it every day for a streak.

        Drills

        Encounters

        Scripted fights: things happen at set moments. The sim cannot follow a script, so it plays a fight of the same length with about as much moving and as many hits, spread evenly.

        Robes

        Earned with your grades in the drills and encounters above. Looks only.

        Seeded fight

        Two minutes on one dummy with the dice fixed by a seed: everyone who types the same seed gets the same rolls, so results can be compared. The box starts with today's date. After the fight, "Copy the result" in the review gives you one line to pass on.

        Lessons

        A short series for the build you play, made from its priority list in the Warlock SIM. Each lesson ends with a test: a short fight with a few things to reach. Pass it and the next lesson opens. Pick another build and its own lessons are here.
